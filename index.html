Questions People Ask

Things you may be wondering.

What happens on a video consultation?

Pick a 15-minute slot between 5 and 8 PM (Mon–Sat), confirm via WhatsApp (7406886226), join from your phone, and leave with a clear next step.

What should I keep ready?

Date of your last period, any scan or lab reports, current medicines or vitamins, and your written questions.

Why are video consultations in the evening?

Dr. Sandesh works at the hospital from 9 AM to 4 PM, so video consultations are held from 5 to 8 PM, Monday to Saturday.

Can I see Dr. Sandesh in person?

Dr. Sandesh sees patients in the OPD at Siddaganga Hospital, Tumakuru, on Thursdays.

Will I need to visit in person?

Many concerns can be discussed on video. If an in-person exam or test is needed, Dr. Sandesh will advise you clearly.

How often should I have check-ups in pregnancy?

Usually monthly in the first six months, every two weeks from 28 weeks, and weekly in the final month, tailored by your doctor.

Is it okay to ask small or simple questions?

Yes, always. Small questions often bring the most peace of mind.

When should I not wait for a video call?

Heavy bleeding, severe pain, fluid leaking, reduced baby movement, severe headache with blurred vision, fits, fainting, chest pain, or trouble breathing require urgent hospital care immediately.
